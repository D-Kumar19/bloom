# Bloom language

Bloom is a way to make and share a personal digital gift through flowers and
words. This glossary describes the experience, not its implementation.

## Language

**Digital bouquet**:
A deliberately chosen arrangement of flower imagery offered as a personal
gesture, not an order for physical flowers.
_Avoid_: Flower delivery, physical bouquet

**Gift**:
The complete personal gesture: digital bouquet, note, presentation, and any
optional sound or personal media.
_Avoid_: Template, transaction

**Occasion starter**:
An editable starting point for a situation or feeling; it suggests choices
without deciding the recipient's preferences or the sender's words.
_Avoid_: Finished gift, automatic love letter

**Keepsake**:
A saved version of a gift that the recipient can revisit.
_Avoid_: Delivery receipt

**Bouquet journal**:
A private collection of gifts a sender has made, presented over time.
_Avoid_: Sent log, delivery history

**Share action**:
An observable action such as copying a link or opening an email composer,
without proof that the gift reached anyone.
_Avoid_: Confirmed send, delivered
